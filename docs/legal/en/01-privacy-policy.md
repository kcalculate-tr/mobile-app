## 2. Scope

This policy covers app users, people who order through eatkcal, visitors of our websites and people who fill in our forms, people who post on Discover, and customers whose marketplace orders (e.g. Trendyol Go) we prepare.

## 4. Health data

- We process the height, weight, goal, measurements and meal logs you enter for calorie and macro tracking **only with your explicit consent**. Consent text: `03-explicit-consent-forms.md` §1.
- This data is **not used for advertising** and is not sent to Meta.
- If you do not consent, calorie tracking cannot work. eatkcal ordering, Discover and other features remain available.
- You can withdraw consent at any time in `[Settings › Privacy › Health data]`. Your health data is then destroyed within 30 days.
- If you share a meal photo as a day card or post, the audience you choose (your followers or everyone) can see it.

> **[Reviewer note]** Whether height/weight/meal logs always qualify as "health data" is debatable. The inventory takes the safest view (special category). See `BOSLUKLAR.md` B-03.

## 5. AI processing

**AI plate scan.** Your meal photo is uploaded to our private storage and sent to **Anthropic PBC's (USA) Claude API** for a nutrition estimate.
- **What is sent:** the photo, a fixed instruction, and an optional short note you type (up to 160 characters).
- **What is not sent:** your name, e-mail or user ID.
- The result is an **estimate**, not medical advice or diagnosis.
- Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs are not used for model training by default.
- We keep scan photos for up to 30 days after analysis.

> **[Reviewer note]** Three points to settle before publication:
> 1. Confirm Anthropic's API input/output retention and its DPA/SCC status from the contract.
> 2. Scan photos are currently kept indefinitely. Do not publish the 30-day statement until the deletion job exists.
> 3. `analyze-ai-scan` and `moderate-content` are file-only (not deployed). This section must take effect before they go live.

**Automated content pre-check.** Discover posts and comments are checked automatically by the same provider before they are published.
- **What is sent:** the post text and up to 6 images or video frames.
- **What is not sent:** the author's identity.

## 6. User content and moderation

- Content you post on Discover is visible to your followers or to everyone, depending on your profile setting. Post media is served via public links.
- Posts are checked automatically before publication. Content that appears to break the rules is not published or is removed.
- You can see the reason for a decision and **appeal** it. A human reviews appeals.
- We keep report and moderation records for 6 months to 1 year.
- Community Guidelines: `07-community-guidelines.md`.

> **[Reviewer note]** Media of private-profile posts is also in a public bucket, so anyone with the link can open it. Until this is fixed, "visible to your followers" is incomplete.

## 7. Ad measurement (Meta) and iOS App Tracking Transparency

- To measure our ads, we send certain in-app events to Meta Platforms (Ireland/USA) **only with your consent**: product view, add to cart, checkout start, purchase (amount, order number) and registration.
- On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is read only if you allow tracking in Apple's ATT prompt. On Android, the advertising ID (GAID) is used only with your in-app consent.
- Health data is **never** sent to Meta.
- You can withdraw consent in `[Settings › Privacy › Ad measurement]`. On iOS you can also do so in device Settings › Privacy › Tracking.
- Consent text: `03-explicit-consent-forms.md` §4.

> **[Reviewer note]** Current code sends events on Android without consent, and keeps SDK auto-logging on iOS after an ATT denial. This text is inaccurate until BOSLUKLAR B-06 is fixed.

## 13. International transfers

Some providers above are outside Türkiye (in the EU and USA), so your data is transferred abroad.

**KVKK (Art. 9, 2024 regime):**
- For regular transfers to countries without an adequacy decision, we sign the **standard contracts** published by the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board, without changes.
- We notify the Authority within **5 business days** of signing each one.
- Only for incidental, non-regular transfers do we rely on explicit consent given after being informed of the risks (Art. 9(6)(a)); see `03-explicit-consent-forms.md` §2.

**GDPR (Chapter V):**
- For transfers outside the EEA, we rely on the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses**.
- For recipients certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, we rely on the adequacy decision.

## 15. Security

- Data is encrypted in transit with TLS.
- Row-level security in the database ensures each user can access only their own data.
- Card tokens are kept in a table accessible only to the server role.
- Staff access is role-based.
- In a breach, we notify the Turkish Board within 72 hours (KVKK Art. 12(5)) and affected people as soon as possible. Where the GDPR applies, we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours (Art. 33) and affected people where required (Art. 34). See `10-data-breach-response-plan.md`.

## 16. Your rights

**Under KVKK Art. 11** you have the right to:
- learn whether your data is processed, and request information if it is;
- learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used for that purpose;
- know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred;
- request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
- request deletion or destruction under Art. 7;
- request that corrections and deletions be notified to the third parties who received the data;
- object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis;
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

**Under the GDPR** you have the right to:
- access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17) and restriction (Art. 18);
- data portability (Art. 20);
- object (Art. 21), and to object to direct marketing at any time;
- not be subject to solely automated decisions (Art. 22);
- withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

**Complaints:** you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Art. 14) and to the supervisory authority in your EU member state (GDPR Art. 77).

**How to make a request:**
- Channels: the in-app form `[Settings › Privacy › Data request]`, `[privacy@…]`, `[KEP]`, or in writing to `[ADDRESS]`.
- Form and process: `08-data-subject-request-form-and-process.md`.
- Under KVKK Art. 13 we respond free of charge **within 30 days at the latest**.
- Under GDPR Art. 12(3) the deadline is **one month**, extendable by two months; we will tell you if we extend it.

**Account deletion:** in the app, go to Profile › Security › Delete my account, or use `eatkcal.com/account-deletion`. What is deleted and what is kept (e.g. de-identified order and financial records) is set out in §14.
