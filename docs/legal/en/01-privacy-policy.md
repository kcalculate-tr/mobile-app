## 5. AI processing

**AI plate scan.** Your meal photo is uploaded to our private storage and sent to **Anthropic PBC's (USA) Claude API** for a nutrition estimate.
- **What is sent:** the photo, a fixed instruction, and an optional short note you type (up to 160 characters).
- **What is not sent:** your name, e-mail or user ID.
- The result is an **estimate**, not medical advice or diagnosis.
- Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs are not used for model training by default.
- We keep scan photos for up to 30 days after analysis.

## 5B. kcalAI Coach

kcalAI Coach (once available) is a chat feature that uses AI to generate nutrition suggestions from what you write and from your daily tracking. Using the Coach is **optional** and is enabled with a separate permission.

**Sent to our AI provider (Anthropic PBC, USA) to generate a reply:**
- your message and the **last 20 messages** of the chat (yours and the Coach's);
- **your daily tracking summary:** gender, daily calorie and macro targets, today's calorie/protein/carb/fat totals, daily calorie and protein totals for the last 7 days, name and nutrition values of your last 8 meals;
- whether you are in the eatkcal delivery area (a yes/no derived from the city of your saved address; the address itself is not sent);
- if you ask about a recipe, scan result or product, its name and nutrition values;
- a fixed instruction and the list of recipes/products that may be suggested.

**Not sent:** your name, e-mail, phone, address, user ID or photos.

**Safety pre-filter:** messages showing signs of a medical condition, medication or an eating disorder are **not sent** to the AI; the Coach replies with a fixed message pointing you to a health professional.

**Retention and deletion:**
- Your chat history (your messages and the Coach's replies) is kept for **30 days**; older messages are deleted.
- You can **delete your whole history** at any time in Coach › History; deletion cannot be undone. Your daily allowance counters are not affected.
- When you delete your account, your chat history is deleted too.

**Permission:** before your first message we ask for the "kcalAI Coach and AI" permission. Without it, no message is sent to the Coach. You can withdraw it in Profile › Privacy › Data and ad permissions (§13A).

Coach replies are not medical advice (Terms of Use §12A.11).

## 13A. International transfers and explicit consent for AI features

When you use AI scan (photo, note, voice-entry text) and kcalAI Coach, the data listed in §5A and §5B is transferred to our AI provider Anthropic PBC in the **USA**; subscription data is transferred to RevenueCat, Inc. in the USA. The Turkish Personal Data Protection Board has not issued an adequacy decision for the USA.

**Under KVKK Art. 9:**
- Because these transfers are **regular**, we sign the Board's **standard contract** with each provider without changes and notify the Authority within **5 business days** of signing.
- Because the data sent to the provider can include information that may qualify as health data (meal photos, nutrition summary), we additionally rely on your **explicit consent** to process it (Art. 6(3)(a)).

**In-app AI permissions (explicit consent mechanism):**
- **"Photo and voice analysis"** permission: asked once before your first AI scan. The sheet states which data is sent, for what purpose, to a provider in which country, and that no audio is recorded.
- **"kcalAI Coach and AI"** permission: asked separately before your first Coach message.
- Both permissions are **optional**, given separately, and have a **"Cancel"** option. Without them you can log meals manually or by barcode; the rest of the app keeps working.
- Your permission is saved to your account with the text version and date. If the permission text changes materially, we ask again.
- You can withdraw either permission at any time in **Profile › Privacy › Data and ad permissions › AI**. Withdrawal takes effect for the future: no further data is sent to the provider; processing before withdrawal remains lawful.

- learn whether your data is processed, and request information if it is;
- learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used for that purpose;
- know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred;
- request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
- request deletion or destruction under Art. 7;
- request that corrections and deletions be notified to the third parties who received the data;
- object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis;
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

**Under the GDPR** you have the right to:
- access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17) and restriction (Art. 18);
- data portability (Art. 20);
- object (Art. 21), and to object to direct marketing at any time;
- not be subject to solely automated decisions (Art. 22);
- withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).
