Short and clear: so everyone can share comfortably.

## 1. Three short rules

1. **Share real nutrition info;** posting false information on purpose is not allowed.
2. **Be respectful to others;** harassment and hate speech are not allowed.
3. **No ads, spam, or sharing someone else's content without permission.**

## 2. What you can share

- **Your food, recipes, kitchen**
- **Sport and training:** gym wear and workout photos are fine
- **Your activities:** runs, walks, matches, classes
- **You:** selfies, a moment from your day

## 3. What we don't publish

| Category | What it means | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Violence and threats** | Content that harms or frightens anyone | threats, glorifying violence, gore, animal cruelty |
| **18+ content** | Nudity and sexual content | nudity, sexually suggestive poses, offers of sexual services. Gym wear and workout photos are not in this category. |
| **Hate speech** | Demeaning a group | demeaning people based on race, ethnicity, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability, age or body |
| **Self-harm** | Content that encourages or describes it; **we show support resources** | methods of self-harm, encouraging starving/purging ("pro-ana/pro-mia"), dangerous fasting and purging "tips" |
| **Spam** | Repetitive, misleading or promotional posts | posting the same thing repeatedly, selling followers, fake giveaways, link bait, scams |
| **Child safety** | Anything that puts children at risk | any content sexualising minors (reported to authorities), revealing images of children without permission |
| **Harassment and bullying** | Attacks targeting a person | insults, persistent unwanted contact, **body shaming**, doxxing |
| **Fake accounts and impersonation** | Pretending to be someone else | accounts in the name of another person, brand or organisation |
| **Illegal content** | Content prohibited by law | sale of illegal substances, weapons or medicines; gambling ads; copyright infringement |

## 4. Nutrition and health information rules

Discover is a nutrition community; what is shared here can affect others.
- **No dangerous diet advice:** don't recommend very-low-calorie diets (e.g. under 800 kcal a day), prolonged fasting, purging, or laxative/diuretic misuse.
- **No promotion of eating disorders:** content presenting extreme thinness as a goal ("thinspiration") is not published.
- **No medical claims:** don't claim a food, supplement or plan cures disease or gives "miracle" results; don't diagnose.
- **False/dangerous nutrition info** can be reported under "Wrong or dangerous nutrition info".
- You can share your personal experience; just remember it isn't right for everyone.

## 5. Privacy

- Don't share someone else's **personal data** (phone, address, ID, health information, weight/measurements) without permission. **Other people's numbers are not yours to share.**
- Don't share someone else's **photo or video** without their permission; take care with images from gyms or restaurants where other people are clearly visible.
- Avoid content that reveals **children's** faces or identities.
- Your meal/activity card may contain health-related information; think before sharing.

## 6. Intellectual property

Only share content you created or have the right to share. Don't use other people's photos, recipes, videos or music without permission. Send infringement notices to `[legal@…]`.

## 7. Advertising and partnership disclosure

If you have a paid or gifted partnership with a brand, say so clearly at the start of the post: **#ad**, **#partnership** or "**gifted by brand X**" (in Turkish: #reklam, #işbirliği). This is required by the Turkish Advertising Board's **Guide on Commercial Advertising and Unfair Commercial Practices by Social Media Influencers** (2021) and equivalent rules elsewhere. Hidden advertising is not allowed.

## 8. Additional rules for creators (kcalculate.ai creator programme)

1. No medical claims, diagnoses or miracle results.
2. Disclose sponsorships and gifted products every time.
3. Real food, real photos; no stock images.
4. No weight-shaming; **no before/after body photos**.
5. Other people's numbers are not yours to share.

## 9. How we moderate

- **Automated check before publishing:** posts and comments are checked by an AI-assisted system before going live. It looks at: violence, sexual content, nudity, hate, self-harm, child safety, spam, other. For this check, text and images are sent to our service provider **without your identity details**.
- **Human review:** our moderators review content when the system is unsure, when content is reported, or when you appeal.
- **Context:** we take news, educational, awareness and artistic context into account.

## 10. Reporting

When you see content that breaks the rules, use the **"Report"** option on the post or comment and choose a reason:
wrong or dangerous nutrition info · body shaming · harassment or bullying · hate speech · spam or scam · fake account · something else.

We don't tell the content owner who reported it. You can **block** a user; people you block cannot see you or your posts. For reports of illegal content you can also write to `[legal@…]`.

## 11. Enforcement

Measures are graduated by seriousness and repetition:
1. Content not published or removed + notice
2. Warning
3. Temporary restriction of posting (e.g. 7 days)
4. Temporary account suspension
5. Account closure

In cases such as **child sexual exploitation, serious threats of violence or serious fraud** we close the account directly and report to the competent authorities where required.

## 12. Appeals

Posts are checked automatically before going live. **If you think a decision is wrong, you can appeal.** Use the appeal option in the app within **6 months** of the decision. Appeals are reviewed by **a human** and you are told the outcome. If the decision was wrong, the content is restored.
